FAQ

Common questions

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO helps a business appear in Google Maps and local search results when nearby customers search for its services. It combines Google Business Profile optimization, local landing pages, citations, reviews and on-site signals.

What does Technical SEO include?

Technical SEO covers the foundations that let search engines crawl, render and index your site: site structure, sitemaps, robots.txt, canonicals, redirects, page speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile rendering and structured data.

How do you approach eCommerce SEO?

I start with product and category keyword research, then improve category and product pages, internal linking, structured data and technical issues such as duplicate variants and faceted URLs. I work with Shopify, WooCommerce and other suitable platforms.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) help your business get found and cited in AI-powered answers, such as AI Overviews and chat-based search. They rely on clear, structured, trustworthy content and strong entity signals.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in AI Overviews or AI answers?

No. Nobody can guarantee inclusion in AI-generated answers. I focus on clear content, reliable information, entity consistency and technical accessibility, which improve your chances without making promises that cannot be kept.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on competition, location and the current state of your website and profile. Many businesses see movement within a few months, but results build over time and no specific ranking can be guaranteed.

Do you guarantee first-page or #1 rankings?

No. Rankings depend on many factors outside anyone's control. I focus on proven, white-hat work and honest reporting, and I will tell you what is realistic for your market.

Do you work with SaaS and ecommerce companies?

Yes. I work with local businesses, SaaS companies and ecommerce brands, including Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento stores.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I work remotely and have delivered projects for clients across multiple countries.

What do you need to get started?

Your website address, a short description of your services and the areas you serve, and your main goal (for example more calls, more orders or more signups). Access to your Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics helps if you have them.

How will I know what is working?

You receive regular reports covering rankings, organic traffic, calls and leads, with a plain-language summary of what changed and what comes next.

What does the free SEO audit include?

A review of your current search presence, including your website basics and Google Business Profile, followed by a short list of the highest-priority opportunities.
